About the Project

This project brings together a curated directory of tribal women writers connected with Jharkhand’s literary and cultural landscape. It highlights writers working in Hindi, Santali, Kharia, Ho, Kurukh and related literary traditions, while recognising that much Adivasi knowledge also lives through oral traditions, songs and community memory.

Languages & Literary Traditions

Jharkhand’s Adivasi literary landscape includes Santali, Mundari, Ho, Kurukh (Oraon), Kharia and Hindi writing. Writers frequently move between languages, print literature, oral traditions, journalism, translation and cultural activism.
